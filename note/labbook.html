Multi-cycle holonomy on POPI

Lab book, 19-20 November 2026. Follow-up to ../dvc-holonomy-pilot (that folder is imported read-only and was not modified). One exposed patient, 28 landmarks, ten breathing phases.

Findings in order of weight
  1. Visibility ceiling. 93.1% of the registration error energy is integrable on the 10-phase graph (median landmark 96.4%); independent edge noise would be 20%. Loops cannot see integrable error, so in this setting holonomy can see at most about 7% of the error energy.
  2. Most of the remaining error is shared by two unrelated instruments. The 32-voxel translation window and whole-volume Diffeomorphic Demons have error vectors with per-axis correlation 0.85, 0.81, 0.90 (z, y, x). Shared part: 1.053 mm RMS. Each instrument's own part: 0.572 mm (refined window), 0.272 mm (Demons).
  3. The shared part behaves like annotation jitter. Its phase-to-phase correlation is about -0.13 at every separation, which is what independent per-point noise gives after demeaning (-0.125). 398 of 400 landmark z values sit on the 2 mm slice grid; snapping alone predicts 0.816 mm pair SD in z, and the shared z SD is 0.768 mm.
  4. Inside its window, the loop residual localises. It names the truly worst edge in 4 to 8 of 28 landmarks across the four runs, versus 0 to 1 for the image residual and 0.6 by chance. It also ranks landmarks for a given phase pair better than the image residual (Spearman 0.198 vs 0.088). The declared localisation criterion (within-landmark Spearman above both comparators) was not met: NCC 0.236, loop 0.124.
  5. DIR-Lab known-answer test: FAIL (pre-registered). Reading registrar covariance as annotation noise did not reproduce DIR-Lab's published repeat-observer error: estimates ran 1.3 to 5.2 times the published value, where the prediction was 0.71 times (pooled ratio 0.36 against a band of 0.94 to 2.12; 0 of 9 cases in band) and tracked motion size. On large-motion extreme phases both registrars share a tracking error, so the shared term is not truth noise there. The POPI attribution in items 2 and 3 therefore rests on its own fingerprints, not on a validated estimator.
  6. Visibility ceiling replicates on DIR-Lab: 79.1% of window-instrument error is loop-invisible on 477 six-phase graphs across 10 patients (chance 33%).
  7. Synchronisation does not improve the phase-10 estimates (-3.6% for holonomy-robust sync, CI [-0.071, -0.006] mm). It improves all-pairs TRE by 2 to 3% and triplet max-of-3 by about 5%. Holonomy-robust sync beats residual-weighted sync by 0.014 mm, CI [0.001, 0.028].

1. Question

Every chapter of the pilot used three observations per query: one independent loop. One loop can flag an inconsistency but cannot say which edge is wrong. POPI has ten phases. The complete graph on ten phases has 45 edges and 36 independent cycles per axis, so the Hodge solver can both synchronise (replace the 45 edges with their integrable part) and localise (each edge's cyclic remainder). This was untested.

2. Method

Instrument unchanged and imported from the pilot: tapered FFT translation on the canonical 32-voxel normalised window, Powell NCC refinement as the secondary variant, the interior/texture guards, and the pilot's dh.hodge.decompose. Same integer centre (the phase-10 landmark) in all ten phases, as in chapter 13. All 45 directed edges i<j measured for each of the 28 eligible landmarks (the same 28 as chapter 13; of the other 12, 9 fail the frozen interior guard in at least one phase and 3 fail the texture check). Measurement never reads target landmarks.

Order of record: PLAN.json and measure.py hashed at 2026-11-19T23:13:06Z; evaluate.py and the measurements hashed at 2026-11-19T23:14:45Z, before its first run; the Lagrangian addendum hashed at 2026-11-19T23:19:59Z, before its run. diagnose.py and shared_error.py are post-hoc, written after the declared results were seen. POPI was already exposed by the pilot, so everything here is exploratory.

Continuity check: the direct edges reproduce chapter 13's FFT triplet figure (1.591 mm) on all 140 records, max difference 3.8e-14 mm. Hodge orthogonality error at most 5.8e-16.

4. Why the loops see so little (post-hoc)

Truth is a set of node positions, so it is exactly integrable. The cyclic part of a measurement is therefore exactly the cyclic part of its error. Hodge-splitting the true error gives the share no loop can see.

Per edge, the median gross edge (>2 mm) has 0.7% of its error in the cyclic part; 4 of 237 gross edges are mostly cyclic. The worst edge per landmark averages 3.612 mm with a median cyclic share of 1.1%. The top-1 hits in section 3 come from those rare isolated failures.

Estimator shrinkage, truth-free. Integer-voxel crop offsets of the phase-10 volume give exact known shifts (308 tests on the 28 landmark windows). Measured/applied gain z, y, x: FFT 0.955, 0.897, 0.899; refined 0.980, 0.922, 0.925. Refinement recovers small shifts exactly and falls back to FFT beyond its 4-voxel support. Against landmarks, the node-error gain is lower (FFT 0.91, 0.66, 0.58), and a per-axis gain explains only 30.9% of node-error energy. Estimator shrinkage is a small part of the integrable error.

These two registrars on DIR-Lab extreme phases are outside that window. The phase-independence secondary was not run (Demons was fitted T00 to T50 only, and the T10 to T40 points are model-propagated).

7. Reading the pilot chapters against these measurements

  • Chapters before 13 (DVC foam scans, acceptance and interior studies, known-motion controls): one loop per query, and the pilot's own calibration found consistent-but-wrong taper bias. That is integrable error, invisible to loops.
  • Chapter 13 (POPI transfer): direct FFT phase-10 TRE 1.074 mm; about 1.05 mm RMS of the error per edge is shared with an unrelated instrument.
  • Chapter 15 (Demons, "18.32% oracle headroom"): the oracle chose between candidates whose star-edge errors correlate at 0.77 to 0.90 per axis, scored against points with slice-snapped z. The selection gain is measured against that truth.

8. Operating window

The loop residual measures edge-specific inconsistency. Measured here: it finds isolated single-edge failures (top-1 worst edge several times above image residual and chance) and ranks landmarks within a phase pair. It carries no information about error shared by all edges of a node: window-versus-landmark tracking difference, estimator gain, or truth noise. On POPI that shared class is about 93% of the error energy, and about 1 mm RMS of it is common to two unrelated registrars.

9. Limits

  • One patient, 28 landmarks, already exposed. All CIs are conditional on this patient.
  • The POPI shared-error attribution to annotation is by fingerprint (phase independence, z grid), not by repeat annotation. Image ambiguity common to both registrars is a co-explanation for part of it. The general estimator (covariance = truth noise) failed its pre-registered known-answer test on DIR-Lab (section 6b).
  • Demons predictions are the pilot's stored values; Demons was run on a finite iteration budget.
  • The Lagrangian centres come from FFT star estimates and inherit their error.
